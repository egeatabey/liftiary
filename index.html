Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between KG and LB?

Triple-tap anywhere on the main screen with three fingers to toggle instantly between kilograms (KG) and pounds (LB). All logged lifts and planned sets convert automatically.

How do gym reminders work?

Tap the bell icon on the main screen to add, edit, or toggle daily motivational reminder times. Once you check off "Went to the gym today" or complete a set, remaining reminders for that day are silenced automatically.

Where is my workout data stored?

All of your data stays 100% locally on your iPhone. No account is required, and no data is ever sent to external servers.

How are Personal Records (PRs) calculated?

Liftiary tracks your best sets and calculates your estimated 1-Rep Max (e1RM) for each exercise. Tap the PRs pill at the bottom of the screen to view all your records.
