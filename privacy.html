Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · App: Liftiary (iOS)

Liftiary is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Data Storage

All workout logs, exercise plans, custom splits, personal records, unit preferences, and reminder schedules you enter into Liftiary are stored strictly locally on your device. Liftiary does not use external servers, cloud accounts, or user registration.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

Liftiary does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers (IDFA), crash logs, or location data. No third-party tracking SDKs or advertising frameworks are included in the app.

3. Notifications

If you choose to enable workout reminders, Liftiary uses Apple's local UserNotifications framework to schedule reminders directly on your device. No push notification tokens or device identifiers are sent to any server.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete individual workouts, sets, or splits inside the app at any time, or remove all app data permanently by deleting Liftiary from your iPhone.

5. Children's Privacy

Liftiary does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If Liftiary adds optional cloud sync or other features that affect data handling in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.
