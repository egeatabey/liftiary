Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · App: Liftiary (iOS)

Liftiary respects your privacy. Your workout logs stay strictly on your device, and we use Google AdMob to display ads that keep the app free.

1. Workout Data Storage

All workout logs, exercise plans, custom splits, personal records, unit preferences, and reminder schedules you enter into Liftiary are stored strictly locally on your device. Liftiary does not require user registration or upload your fitness data to external servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Liftiary displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect certain device and diagnostic information, such as approximate location, device identifiers (including Apple's Advertising Identifier / IDFA if you grant permission via App Tracking Transparency), and ad interaction data.

You can control personalized ad tracking at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Notifications

If you choose to enable workout reminders, Liftiary uses Apple's local UserNotifications framework to schedule reminders directly on your device. No push notification tokens are sent to any server.

4. Data Deletion

Because your workout data is stored locally on your device, you can delete individual workouts, sets, or splits inside the app at any time, or remove all local app data permanently by deleting Liftiary from your iPhone.

5. Children's Privacy

Liftiary is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
